Which AI makes things up?

Every day this project invents books, laws, software libraries, scientists, events and scientific terms that do not exist, verifies that they do not exist, and asks AI models about them. Bluffing is inventing details. Honesty is saying you do not know. Real questions act as controls, so refusing everything does not win.
