# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can disagree about a benchmark without
attacking each other. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

* Being specific about a technical disagreement: cite the trap id, the answer text, the label.
* Accepting correction when a trap turns out to be real, or a score turns out to be wrong.
* Assuming good faith about a measurement you disagree with, and asking how it was produced.
* Keeping criticism aimed at methods, not at people or at the teams who build the models.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* Insults, mockery, or "your model is stupid" style comments about any model or company.
* Harassment, discrimination, or sexual attention of any kind.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Submitting traps about real people, real companies, real diseases, or anything that could
  cause harm if repeated as fact.
* Deliberately degrading the benchmark: editing raw answers, cherry-picking samples, or
  presenting demo data as real results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or reject contributions that violate this code.
Repeated or serious violations lead to a ban from the repository. Reports can be sent to the
maintainer through GitHub; they are read in confidence.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any space where
someone is representing the project.
